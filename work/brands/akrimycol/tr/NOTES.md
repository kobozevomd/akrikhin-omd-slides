# Akrimycol deck — translator notes (in addition to STYLE_GUIDE_EN.md and glossary_en.md)

Brand: Akrimycol (Akrikhin) — antifungal cream for skin fungal infections (athlete's foot / tinea pedis, tinea versicolor, ringworm). Today ~50% of sales come via doctors' prescriptions; in 2027 HCP promo is cut, so digital must move the choice to consumer self-choice. Competitors: Zalain, Exoderil, Oflomil, Fungoderil, Mizol Evalar, Rozilak, Lamisil (TV case 2004–2015), Termikon, Mikoderil, Oflomikol. Readers: Akrikhin marketing team.
